Starting your own Medical Practise

You are taking your first step as a Clinician Owner and managing your own Practise! You have a couple options and variations on how the operation can work for you. Generally speaking the larger the space/practise the greater the passive income that you can attain. With that said there is slightly more risk and longer upswing to hitting all your numbers and financial milestones. Smaller spaces are easy to get into, have low costs, but also lower passive income potential. Property ownership can be very profitable if done correctly. It has higher risks, but much higher rewards. When done properly you have the potential to double (or more) the value of the property. With that said, finding the right property is not easy and this process take a lot longer than leasing.

Here are the steps to getting yourself into a space with timelines and associated costs

Why do I need legal representation?

When ever entering into a lease you need legal representation to review your lease and obligations. We are not lawyers and can’t represent you on finalizing the lease. This is something that requires a licensed lawyer. If you don’t have a lawyer we can refer you to one. Think of legal representation as insurance and risk mitigation on a transaction.

Why do I need a zoning certificate?

A zoning certificate is the first step in ensuring that you are able to build your space. Its a requirement by every city and township. We also put this language into an offer to ensure that you can walk away from a space if zoning does not permit the use we are looking for. The cost of the zoning certificate is generally dependant on the size of the unit. There are also fee’s associated with submitting an application by the city and/or township.

  • Timelines to find a space: 1 Week to 50 weeks. Depends on the framework of finding a space.
  • Timelines to negotiate a space: 1-2 Weeks, mainly depending on the timelines of the landlord and their quickness to respond.
  • Timelines for Legal: 2-4 Weeks once they have received a full form lease
  • Timelines for zoning Certificate: 4-6 weeks depending on the city timelines

Why should I sub-lease to tenants?

In short, its the most cost efficient way to manage a practise. You will have tenants who will support in leaseholds and rent costs. For more nuance, please set up a call/meeting to discuss.

Do I have to sub lease? Can I hire Associates?

For some healthcare professionals you will need to sub-rent, for example Pharmacists. For others you are able to hire or sub-rent, for example Physiotherapists. There are pro’s and cons to structuring these agreements. For details on the best path forward for you practise please set up a call/meeting to formalize your specific strategy

Timelines to procure sub-tenants: 3-6 weeks. Please keep in mind that this will start at the same time as finding a space (concurrently) so its not an additional time constraint.

How will this work?

Firstly you will provide your needs list with a wants list. We will consult you on this process and ask all the right questions to ensure we get everything right. We will discuss room orientation, office space, charting rooms, nursing stations, reception, stage areas and every other detail on ensure you are able to get the space you need to manage your practise.
